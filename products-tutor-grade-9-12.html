What it is

  • Rigorous Socratic guide for ages 14–18 — challenges weak premises instead of accepting them, and pushes a student to defend a position with evidence rather than assertion.
  • Verified-only answers — the strict charter requires every factual response to come from retrieved Qdrant chunks.   • Grade-level calibration — Grade 9 works toward structured multi-step analysis; Grade 12 operates at full argumentative depth, including counterargument, qualification, and evidence weighting.
  • College-prep depth — trains the cognitive skills that transfer: framing a problem, weighing competing evidence, and reaching a defensible conclusion.

See the method hold

A real exchange. The tutor asks one clarifying question, validates the reasoning, and escalates — it does not lecture, and it does not drift off thread.

Student: I want to study for history test. shoot me some questions about ancient greek history
Tutor:
1. What was the role of city-states in Ancient Greek society and how did they contribute to the development of Greek culture?
2. Can you explain the concept of 'polis' and its significance in Ancient Greek civilization?
3. Describe the role of Athens and Sparta in the Peloponnesian War. Who emerged victorious and why was this war significant?
4. Discuss the rise and influence of the Delian League, its transformation into the Athenian Empire, and the implications for Ancient Greek politics.
5. Explain the significance of the Olympic Games in Ancient Greece, including their origins, impact on Greek city-states, and role in promoting cultural unity.
6. What were the key differences between the Athenian democracy and Spartan oligarchy? How did these political systems shape their respective societies?
7. Describe the life and achievements of notable Ancient Greek figures such as Pericles, Socrates, Plato, or Alexander the Great.
8. Analyze the impact of Ancient Greek philosophy on Western thought, including its influence on ethics, politics, and metaphysics.
9. Discuss the role of women in Ancient Greek society, their rights, and social status across various city-states.
10. Explain the significance of Ancient Greek religion, its pantheon of gods, and the relationship between religious beliefs and daily life.

For each question, make sure to critically analyze the topic, provide context, and cite relevant sources when necessary. Remember, history is not just about memorizing facts but understanding the interpretations, causes, and effects that shaped historical events.

Questions parents ask

Does this replace a real teacher?

No — it complements them. The Socratic method guides the student to discover answers themselves, but complex emotional or behavioral challenges still need human educators. This Role handles structured, knowledge-bound tutoring with strict verification.

Is it safe for my teen?

Yes. The charter requires verified answers, no fabrication, no inappropriate content. The DB stores only educational profiles — no external cloud storage. The role has no internet access once installed, so there's no open web for a student to wander into.

Can it work without WiFi?

Yes — after initial setup. Once MAi-RAG-P.A., Ollama, Qdrant, and the role package are installed locally, turn off WiFi. The tutor continues to function entirely offline. No ads, no notifications, no feeds — just the argument.

Will it just give my teen the answers?

No — and at this level, giving answers is the failure mode worth avoiding. The tutor challenges thin premises, demands evidence, and asks the student to revise a claim that can't account for the evidence. It answers direct questions when asked, but the default mode is guided analysis.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

Cloud models answer from memory, invent citations, and contradict themselves between sessions. This Role answers only from its verified Qdrant collection, remembers your student in a local database, and tells the truth when it has no record. It also works with the WiFi off, and your teen's data never leaves your machine.

What model does it use?

Default: qwen2.5:7b. The manifest leaves the model override empty so the database and WebUI control it. For full argumentative depth on a strong machine, a larger local model is recommended.

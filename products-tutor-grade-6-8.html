What it is

  • Framework-bound by a strict charter — the Strict Non-Hallucinatory Charter requires answers to come from retrieved Qdrant chunks. If zero chunks match, it says exactly: "I don't have that information in my records." No fabricated sources, no invented prior sessions.

  • Grade-level calibration — automatic tone shift: Grade 6 leans on concrete reasoning, Grade 8 expects multi-step analytical reasoning and follows an argument through to a conclusion.

Questions parents ask

Does this replace a real teacher?

No — it complements them. The Socratic method guides the student to discover answers themselves, but complex emotional or behavioral challenges still need human educators. This Role handles structured, knowledge-bound tutoring with strict verification.

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. The charter requires verified answers, no fabrication, no inappropriate content. The DB stores only educational profiles — no external cloud storage. The role has no internet access once installed.

Can it work without WiFi?

Yes — after initial setup. Once MAi-RAG-P.A., Ollama, Qdrant, and the role package are installed locally, turn off WiFi. The tutor continues to function entirely offline. No distractions, no notifications, no ads — just guided learning.

Will it just give my child the answers?

No. That is the whole design. The tutor asks clarifying questions and validates reasoning instead of handing over solutions — the exact habit that builds independent problem-solving. It answers direct questions when the student asks, but the default mode is guided thinking.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

Cloud models answer from memory, invent sources, and forget your child between sessions. This Role answers only from its verified Qdrant collection, remembers your child in a local database, and tells the truth when it has no record. It also works with the WiFi off, and your child's data never leaves your machine.

What happens with a tricky philosophical question?

The tutor holds the thread and validates the student's interpretation, then escalates the question. It does not dodge. But it also does not fabricate — if the knowledge base returns nothing on the specific claim, the charter requires the tutor to say it has no information in its records rather than inventing support.

The Problem With "DIY" Role Creation

MAi-RAG-PA is open source. You can download it today and start building.
Here's what most users discover within the first week:

What they expected What actually happens
"I'll just paste my PDFs and it works." Chunk size, overlap, and metadata tagging must match the document structure — a legal contract needs different chunking than a textbook.
"I'll write a quick system prompt." Hallucination prevention requires conditional citation rules, anti-fabrication clauses, and strict role boundaries — generic prompts fail on day two.
"I'll use whatever model is installed." A 30B MoE model works for research synthesis but chokes on rapid Q&A; a 7B model is fast but misses nuance. Matching model to workload is critical.
"It'll remember what I tell it." Role-scoped memory must be seeded, tagged, and isolated — otherwise facts leak between roles and corrupt the specialist's context.

Role Creation is not a configuration task. It is a data engineering and prompt architecture task.
We do the engineering. You get the specialist.

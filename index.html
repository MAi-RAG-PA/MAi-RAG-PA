Your AI Just Became a Team.

MAi-RAG-P.A. v1.7.0 introduces Roles — self-contained AI specialists, each with its own private knowledge base, learned memory, personality, and rules. One assistant, many experts. All local, all private.

What Is a Role?

A Role bundles a system prompt, a private Qdrant collection, its own memory, a citation setting, and an optional model override. Switch Roles and the entire assistant transforms — knowledge, tone, and memory included.

Role-Scoped Isolation

Facts learned as well as content in the "Tax Preparation" role never leak into the "Creative Writing" role. Client A's contracts or information never surface in Client B's thread. This is a compliance feature, not just convenience.

Role Creation Is a Craft

The framework is free. Building a good Role requires dataset engineering, prompt architecture, hallucination defense, and model matching. That's where we come in.

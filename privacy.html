Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

MAi-RAG-PA is built on the principle that your data belongs to you. This policy explains what information we collect on this website, why, and how it's handled.

Short version: This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We collect only what's necessary to respond to your inquiries, deliver free samples, and process purchases. We never sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

MAi-RAG-PA is an open-source local AI framework. This website (mai-rag-pa.github.io/MAi-RAG-PA) is our informational site and storefront. For privacy matters, contact us via the form at roles.html#contact.

2. What we collect and why

Contact form submissions

When you submit the Role Request form, we collect:

  • Your name and email address (to reply to you)
  • The Role you're requesting and any details you provide
  • Document type and count preferences you select

This is used solely to respond to your inquiry and scope your project. Submissions are processed by Formspree (formspree.io), which forwards them to our email inbox.

Free sample requests

When you request a free sample, we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Which sample you requested

This is used to track interest in our work and (occasionally) to notify you of related product updates. You may request removal at any time.

Paid product purchases

When you buy a digital product, payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe (stripe.com). We do not see or store your credit card details. We receive from Stripe:

  • Your email address (for delivery)
  • Your name (if you provide it at checkout)
  • Purchase confirmation

3. What we DO NOT collect

  • We DO NOT use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or ANY third-party tracking.
  • We DO NOT set marketing cookies or persistent identifiers.
  • We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers.
  • We DO NOT track you across other websites.

4. Third-party services we rely on

Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Formspree — processes contact and sample request forms. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — processes payments. Stripe Privacy Policy

5. Data retention

Contact and sample submissions are retained in our email inbox indefinitely unless you request deletion. Purchase records are retained by Stripe per their standard retention policies (typically 7 years for tax compliance).

6. Your rights

You may request at any time:

  • A copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of any future communications

To exercise these rights, contact us via the contact form and mention "Privacy Request" in your message.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. The only state we store is temporary (e.g., form validation), and it's cleared when you close the tab.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us for removal.

9. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. Form submissions and payments are transmitted over encrypted connections. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes take effect when posted on this page. Material changes will be noted at the top of this document.

11. Jurisdiction

This website is operated from the united states. Any disputes shall be governed by the laws of that jurisdiction.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use our contact form — we reply within 1–2 business days.

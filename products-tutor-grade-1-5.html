Questions parents ask

Does this replace a real teacher?

No — it complements them. The cognizant Socratic method guides the student to discover answers themselves, but complex emotional or behavioral challenges still need human educators. This Role handles structured, knowledge-bound practice with strict verification.

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. The charter requires verified answers, forbids fabrication, and blocks inappropriate content. The database stores only educational profiles. Nothing is written to an external service, and the role has no internet access once installed.

Can it work without WiFi?

Yes — after initial setup. Once MAi-RAG-P.A., Ollama, Qdrant, and the role package are installed locally, turn off WiFi. The tutor keeps functioning. No ads, no notifications — just guided thinking.

Will it just give my child the answers?

No. That is the whole design. The tutor asks clarifying engaging questions and validates reasoning instead of handing over solutions — the exact habit that builds independent problem solving. It answers direct questions when the child asks, but the default mode is guided thinking.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers from memory and can invent sources or disagree with itself between sessions. This Role answers only from its verified database and prompt, remembers your child in the local database, and tells the truth when it has no record. It also works with the WiFi off, and your child's data never leaves your machine.

What model does it use?

Default: qwen2.5:7b. The manifest leaves the model override empty so the database and WebUI control it. You can switch to a larger local model if your hardware supports it.
